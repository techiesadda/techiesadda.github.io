Tata Avinya EV: Tata’s Flagship Born-EV Reimagining Luxury Mobility

By Auto Desk • 5 min read • August 2026
The Tata Avinya features a futuristic front lightbar shaping the iconic Tata emblem.

Tata Motors is preparing to launch its next-generation flagship crossover, the **Tata Avinya EV**. Built as a pure Born-Electric Vehicle (BEV), the Avinya merges a catamaran-inspired crossover design with a serene, screen-minimal luxury cabin.

1. Architecture, Fast Charging & 500+ Km Range

Underpinning the Avinya is Jaguar Land Rover's (JLR) modular **Electrified Modular Architecture (EMA)**. This dedicated born-EV platform maximizes interior floor space while supporting high-voltage battery technology.

  • Driving Range: Targeted real-world driving range of over **500 km** on a single charge.
  • Ultra-Fast Charging: Supports high-capacity DC fast charging that delivers up to 500 km of range in under **30 minutes**.
  • Motor Layout: Expected to launch in both single-motor RWD and dual-motor AWD options.
Streamlined side crossover silhouette with short overhangs and large aerodynamic wheels.

2. Interior Features & Screen-Minimalist Design

Tata has designed the Avinya's cabin around digital detox and acoustic relaxation:

  • Butterfly Doors: Suicide-style doors with no center B-pillar for effortless cabin entry.
  • Screen-Free Dashboard: Replaces bulky infotainment touchscreens with a minimalist, driver-focused steering console bar.
  • Personal Audio Headrests: Integrated soundbar speakers positioned directly inside each seat headrest.

3. Estimated Price & Launch Timeline

The production version of the Tata Avinya is scheduled to debut around **early 2027**. Positioned as Tata's flagship luxury EV, it is expected to carry an ex-showroom price tag between **₹35 Lakh and ₹40 Lakh**.

4. Comparison: Tata Avinya EV vs. Mahindra BE 6e

While Tata focuses on luxury lounge comfort and JLR platform engineering, Mahindra's **BE 6e** focuses on aggressive sci-fi coupe styling and instant performance.
