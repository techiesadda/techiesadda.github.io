Groww vs. Angel One: Which Trading Platform Should You Choose in 2026?

By Rohan Mehta • 6 min read • August 5, 2026
Groww

For Beginners

VS
Angel One

For Active Traders

Choosing between Groww and Angel One comes down to what kind of market player you are: a beginner seeking extreme simplicity, or an active trader looking for algorithmic support and advisory tools. We break down both platforms across convenience, charges, UI design, and exclusive features.

1. Convenience & Ease of Account Setup

Both platforms offer a 100% paperless onboarding process via Digilocker and Aadhaar-based OTP verification, allowing users to activate a Demat account in under 10–15 minutes.

  • Groww: Known for the smoothest onboarding experience in the fintech industry. Perfect for complete beginners, the app guides you step-by-step with zero confusing financial jargon.
  • Angel One: Highly streamlined digitized onboarding. Because Angel One gives access to Commodities and Currencies right away, income proof verification (bank statement/ITR) is requested during setup for segment activation.

2. Platform Charges Comparison

While both brokers follow discount pricing models with capped fees, key differences exist in annual maintenance and equity delivery structures.

Fee / Segment Groww Angel One
Account Opening Fee ₹0 (Free) ₹0 (Free)
Annual Maintenance (AMC) ₹0 Lifetime Free ₹0 (1st Year), then ₹60/qtr + GST
Equity Delivery Brokerage Lower of ₹20 or 0.1% Lower of ₹20 or 0.1%
Equity Intraday Brokerage Lower of ₹20 or 0.05% Lower of ₹20 or 0.03%
Futures & Options (F&O) Flat ₹20 / order Flat ₹20 / order
DP Charges (on Sell) ₹18.50 + GST ₹20.00 + GST

Verdict at a Glance

Groww is best for passive investors & SIP buyers who want zero AMC. Angel One is built for active F&O traders wanting research & algo tools.

3. UI Differences & Extra Platform Features

Groww Interface & Experience

Groww adopts a minimalist design philosophy similar to modern consumer tech apps. It focuses on clean navigation, clear search functionality, and seamless switching between Stocks, Mutual Fund SIPs, and US Investing.

  • Minimalist Dashboard: Uncluttered views for tracking total portfolio value, daily gains, and mutual fund performance.
  • All-in-One Personal Finance: Direct investment in Direct Mutual Funds with 0% commission and Credit Score checks integrated.

Angel One Interface & Extra Features

Angel One is structured as a full-suite trading powerhouse with a feature-dense UI, tailored for day traders and technical analysts.

  • ARQ Prime Advisory Engine: An AI-driven, rule-based recommendation tool that suggests stock picks based on machine learning models.
  • SmartAPI Integration: Free REST API access allowing traders to build custom trading algorithms and automated strategies.
  • Multi-Segment Support: Full access to Commodities (MCX) and Currency derivatives, which are not currently available on Groww.

Both platforms represent top-tier choices in India's fintech landscape. Choose Groww for a hassle-free, zero-maintenance experience, or opt for Angel One if you require advanced research, commodities trading, or API-based trading automation.
